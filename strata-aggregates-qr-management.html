Terminology Note
Within site operations, QR deployment points may be referred to as Tags. Hold recognises both terms interchangeably where aligned with local operational language.
  1. QR deployment points may be referred to locally as tags where aligned with site operational terminology
  2. Use weather-resistant labels for exposed external areas
  3. Fit rigid backing plates where tags are mounted on rough surfaces
  4. Position QR tags away from direct mud, spray and aggregate dust where possible
  5. Confirm scan readability during weekly point inspection
  6. Replace damaged or obscured tags before operational guidance becomes unreliable
